💡 Tactical Shootout Analysis

In the preliminary 5 matches, teams play Net Better Ball. In Better Ball, a player with a 20+ handicap can blow up on a hole without hurting the team if their partner makes a par. However, in the 4-Hole Shootout, the format changes to Modified Alternate Shot.

In Alternate Shot, both partners must hit key approach and putting shots under pressure. The S2 Native Grasses and trees on Holes 9, 16, 17, and 18 are designated penalty areas. Teams from lower handicap flights (Carrick, Nest, Heron) who possess two consistent ball-strikers have a massive statistical advantage over teams with extreme handicap splits (e.g., a scratch golfer paired with a 23 index), as the weaker golfer cannot be shielded!

Handicap & Probability Methodology

1. Net Better Ball Synergy (Flight Matches Stage)

In the five 9-hole flight matches, strokes are determined by dividing all handicaps in half and reducing the lowest handicap in each foursome to zero. Players receive strokes where they fall on the scorecard. Statistical modeling shows that two players with complementary mid-level handicaps (e.g., two 10-12 index players) create a higher net-par/birdie frequency than a scratch player paired with a 20+ index, because stroke coverage is distributed across 6 to 7 holes per 9-hole match.

2. The Modified Alternate Shot Penalty (Shootout Stage)

When flight winners advance to the 4-hole shootout (Holes 9, 16, 17, 18), the format changes to Modified Alternate Shot. Our simulation applies a heavy "volatility penalty" to teams with high individual handicaps or large handicap spreads. In Alternate Shot, a weaker player cannot be hidden. With S2 Native Grasses and treed areas playing as penalty areas (Rule 17.1), teams from Gold/Blue tees with tight low-handicap pairings have up to a 3.5x higher survival rate per elimination hole.

3. Tie-Breaking & Handicap Differential Advantage

As per tournament booklet rules, if teams tie for flight winners or overall standings, ties are broken based on the Member's handicap differential (the team with the higher-handicapped member wins the tie). This gives an explicit mathematical tie-break advantage to teams anchored by higher-index members who manage to tie points.

4. Value Rating & Fair Odds Calculation

Fair American Odds are calculated directly from the true simulated winning probability ($Odds = (100 / Prob) - 1$ converted to moneyline). The "Value Rating" (Stars) identifies market inefficiencies where public parimutuel betting typically undervalues consistent mid-handicap teams or dark horses with high tie-break equity.
